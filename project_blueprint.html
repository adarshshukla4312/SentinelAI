📌 Executive Overview & Core Problem Imperative

Border checkpoints across India process over 72 million international passengers annually. Criminal syndicates and illegal migration networks ("Donkey Routes") deploy sophisticated forgery: spliced visa stamps, digitally altered photos, modified birth dates, and identity impersonation. Manual verification suffers from severe human cognitive fatigue (14% to 20% error rate after 90 minutes of shift duty). Meanwhile, foreign commercial SaaS vendors (Jumio, Onfido) charge \$1.50–\$5.00/scan, compromise data sovereignty by routing biometric PII overseas, and fail completely on India's #1 fraud vector: manipulated black-and-white photocopies (Xerox).

3 What Exactly We Will Be Doing: The 5 Core Engines

The platform executes five modular engines engineered for high accuracy, explainability, and speed:

📄 Module 1: GPU OCR & Checksum Auto-Correction

  • PaddleOCR v4 on CUDA: Rapid multilingual text extraction (English, Hindi, regional scripts) in ~25ms.
  • Dual-Zone Extraction: Concurrently reads the Visual Inspection Zone (VIZ) and Machine Readable Zone (MRZ).
  • Deterministic Checksum Auto-Correction: Uses ICAO 9303 modulo-10 weights (7, 3, 1) to mathematically auto-resolve common OCR misreads (distinguishing `O` vs `0`, `I` vs `1`, and `B` vs `8`).
  • Perspective & Glare Preprocessing: 4-point contour homography deskew + CLAHE illumination equalization.

🔒 Module 2: Deterministic Document Validation

  • Aadhaar RSA-2048 Secure QR Decryption: Offline parsing of compressed binary QR codes and UIDAI public key digital signature verification.
  • Triangular Consistency Cross-Check: Mathematically cross-validates 3 independent anchors:
    • 1. Decrypted QR demographic text
    • 2. Card printed OCR text
    • 3. Embedded QR portrait vs. Card face photo
  • ICAO Mod-10 Composite Validation: Validates TD1, TD2, and TD3 passport checksum sequences and expiry date logic.
Figure 2: Aadhaar Triangular Zero-Trust Architecture (100% Mathematical Proof)

Zero-Trust Integrity Rule: If decrypted QR data does not match the printed OCR text, or if the QR thumbnail does not match the card portrait via ArcFace cosine similarity, the card is flagged with 100% mathematical certainty.

🔬 Module 3: Passive Forensics

  • Halftone Texture: Laplacian + LBP filters expose cut-and-paste splices on B&W photocopies (Xerox).
  • Error Level Analysis (ELA): Exposes JPEG DCT re-compression inconsistencies.
  • SRM Residuals: Spatial Rich Model high-pass kernels expose photo cut boundaries.
  • Copy-Move Detector: SIFT keypoints + RANSAC flag cloned document stamps.

👤 Module 4: Live Face Biometrics

  • ArcFace MobileFaceNet: 512-d embeddings on CUDA in <15ms; matches document photo to live webcam.
  • MiniFASNet Anti-Spoof: Passive liveness flags printouts, screen replays, and masks without prompts.
  • Qdrant Watchlist: Sub-ms vector search against blacklist databases to identify syndicate impostors.

⚖️ Module 5: Fusion & Ledger

  • Bayesian Risk Aggregator: Combines deterministic, forensic, and biometric scores into a 0–100 scale.
  • BSA 2023 Sec 63 PDF: Court-admissible forensic evidence report with SHA-256 digital seals.
  • Blockchain Ledger: Cryptographic receipt chaining preventing insider evidence tampering.

4 Engineering-Impressive Innovations & Blockchain Integration

To capture top honors at SIH, the platform goes far beyond generic computer vision into mathematically proven cryptographic trust and cutting-edge forensic signal processing:

A. Blockchain-Anchored Audit Ledger (BSA 2023 Section 63)

A frequent challenge raised by defense juries is: "What stops a corrupt border official or internal database administrator from altering the scan log to let a suspect pass?"

⛓️ Cryptographic Receipt Chaining

Every screening event generates an immutable cryptographic receipt:

ReceiptHash = SHA256(DocHash + Verdict + OfficerID + Timestamp + PrevReceiptHash)

Receipts are aggregated into an hourly Merkle Tree whose Merkle Root is anchored onto an immutable blockchain ledger (local EVM / Polygon testnet).

⚖️ Court-Admissible Legal Chain of Custody

Under Section 63 of Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (replacing Section 65B of the Indian Evidence Act), electronic evidence requires proof of integrity from the time of creation. Our blockchain receipts provide mathematical non-repudiation that stands up in any court of law.

B. Solving the "Xerox / Photocopy Problem" (India's #1 Fraud Vector)

Over 70% of document forgery in India occurs on black-and-white photocopies. Standard Error Level Analysis (ELA) fails because the physical rescan creates a uniform compression layer. We deploy Halftone Micro-Texture Continuity Analysis:

  • When forged text or photographs are physically spliced onto a document and photocopied, toner particle scattering and edge gradient transitions exhibit micro-discontinuities.
  • By combining Laplacian of Gaussian (LoG) with Local Binary Patterns (LBP), our spatial filter instantly exposes cut-and-paste boundaries that are invisible to the naked human eye.
